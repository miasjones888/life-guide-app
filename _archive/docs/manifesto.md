# Manifesto: Field Guide to Yourself

This document is the single source of truth for the app's philosophy, tone, and content rules.
All agents and the in-app assistant should refer to this file when generating content for Mia.

---

## Who This Is For

**Mia Jones.** Writer, copywriter, creative director. San Diego. Lives with partner Dar and three cats (Maisie, Meeko, and a third). Depression, anxiety, CPTSD, ADHD. Significant task initiation difficulty. Variable energy day to day. Highly self-aware; has built her own life system to work *with* her neurodivergence, not against it.

This app is her life system. It holds everything she would otherwise have to carry in her head.

---

## What This App Is

A mobile-first personal life guide. It lives like a journal. It reads like a guidebook.

Not a productivity dashboard. Not a task manager. Not a Notion clone. Not a habit tracker.

It is a **read-first, reference-first** tool. Using it should feel like consulting a trusted field guide written by someone who knows Mia's life — not interacting with an automated system.

---

## Core Philosophy

**Reduce friction, not demand compliance.**
The app makes the path of least resistance the right path. No reminders, no notifications, no shame for doing the minimum.

**Non-negotiable on whether, flexible on which.**
Some things happen every day. How they happen is flexible.

**Support variability without shame.**
Good days and hard days are equally valid. The hard-day version of anything is enough. The app never asks "did you do this?"

**Everything is written down.**
Mia can trust the system to hold the details so she doesn't have to carry them in her head.

**Editorial, not metric.**
This is a field guide, not a dashboard. Reading it should feel like consulting a trusted reference, not checking a score. No progress bars, no streaks, no gamification.

**Treat content as sacred.**
Every word in this guide is intentional. Preserve Mia's exact voice and phrasing. Never simplify for convenience.

**Nature inside the machine.**
OS window chrome contains organic content. The tool is humane, specific, and built around Mia's actual life — not a generic productivity template.

---

## Tone

**Spare, warm, non-compliance.**

- Short sentences. No filler.
- Warm without being sentimental.
- Never instructional or authoritative.
- Never cheerful, corporate, or motivational.
- Reads like a personal field guide entry, not a productivity notification.
- No greetings. No sign-offs. No "Great job!" No "You've got this!"
- When in doubt: say less.

## Hard-Day Protocol

On low-capacity days, the minimum is enough. The hard-day version of any routine is a complete and valid version. The app never implies otherwise.

For freeze mode: offer one immediate action, 5–10 minutes. Nothing overwhelming. No list of things. One next step, stated gently.

---

## What Agents Must Never Do

- Add metrics, scores, or progress indicators
- Use motivational language ("You're doing great!", "Almost there!")
- Add greetings or sign-offs
- Imply that doing less is failure
- Change or paraphrase verbatim copy
- Add content that wasn't asked for
- Use a tone that is cheerful, corporate, or instructional
