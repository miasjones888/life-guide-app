## §05 Body

Your body is part of the system. Not a project. Not a compliance zone.
These are the practices that keep the engine running.

### Skincare

**Morning (8:00am)**

Full version:
1. Cleanser
2. Toner (if available)
3. SPF 30+ — non-negotiable regardless of plans

Hard-day version:
Cleanser + SPF. On a hard day: just those two. Done.

**Evening (9:45pm)**

Full version:
1. Cleanser
2. Toner
3. Treatment (retinol, vitamin C, or active — not both)
4. Moisturiser

Hard-day version:
Cleanser + moisturiser. Two steps minimum.

### Movement

Outside time is scheduled Thursday at 2:00pm.
This is not a workout. It is a single block: body + spirituality + feedstock in one block. Don't plan it to death. Just go outside.

What counts:
- Hiking (Cowles Mountain, Kwaay Paay, Mission Trails)
- Paddleboarding (Mission Bay)

Non-negotiable on whether, flexible on which.

### Shower / Hygiene

Shower check-in runs on a 2-day interval. It is not daily. That is intentional.

Hard-day protocol: Get in. Warm water. Body wash. Get out. That is the whole task. Nothing else is required.

No temperature, duration, or product requirements on hard days.

### Meals

The goal is fuel, not performance.

**Breakfast (9:15am)**
No appetite is okay — grab something small from the shelf. Nuts, crackers, fruit, whatever is available. Eating something is the task.

**Midday check-in (1:00pm)**
"Eaten today?" — a prompt, not a guilt trigger. If the answer is no, the task is: eat something right now. Shelf snack counts.

**Dinner (6:30pm)**
You don't have to cook. You just have to eat something.
Options in priority of friction:
1. Delivery (DoorDash, UberEats — use gift cards when available)
2. Frozen (keep Trader Joe's meals stocked — check every 2 weeks)
3. Shelf (crackers, beans, pasta, whatever)
4. Cook (when energy permits — not a requirement)

### Sleep

Lights out: 11:00pm.

If sleep is difficult:
- PRN anxiety meds are physically accessible — use them when needed, no guilt
- The lights-out time is a target, not a rule

## §06 Home

The home system is designed to be low-effort and rotating, not comprehensive.
You are not cleaning the whole space. You are cycling through zones.

### Room Reset (5:30pm · Tuesday + Saturday)

Duration: 20 minutes.
This is a rotating phase system — not the same room twice in a row.

Phase rotation:
1. Bedroom surfaces (desk, nightstand, floor)
2. Living room (couch area, surfaces, floor sweep)
3. Kitchen (counters, dishes, basic wipe-down)
4. Bathroom (sink, toilet, floor)
5. Entry + hall

Pick up where you left off. If you can't remember, start with bedroom.

This is not deep cleaning. This is surface order. Deep cleaning is separate and scheduled.

## §08 System

The system is the container for everything else.
This section is for maintenance, review, and reflection — not daily operation.

### Weekly Reset (Sunday, 7:00pm)

Duration: 30–45 minutes.

What happens:
1. Transfer notes from the week → notecards (Sunday, 6:45pm)
2. Review the week: what happened, what shifted, what worked
3. Set the anchor task for Monday
4. Check upcoming deadlines and events
5. Instacart order (Sunday, 6:30pm) — stock for the week

This is not a planning session. It is a clearing session.
You are not making decisions. You are writing down what's already decided.

---

### Monthly Review (First Sunday, 9:00am)

Duration: 90 minutes.

The Monthly Reset + Budget Hour.

Part 1 — Life Review (30 min):
- What changed this month?
- What was harder than expected?
- What worked?

Part 2 — Budget Hour (60 min):
1. Review last month — check all accounts, note surprises
2. Subscription audit — cancel anything unused in 30 days
3. Upcoming obligations — note fixed bills for next 30 days
4. Monthly intention — one financial goal
5. Buffer check — is there a 2-week buffer? If not, pause discretionary

Cat supply check runs immediately after (1:00pm).

---

### Reflection

The reflection space is freeform. No prompts required. No format required.
Write what is true right now.

Prompts to use when stuck:
- What am I carrying right now that isn't mine to carry?
- What worked this week that I didn't give myself credit for?
- What does the system need that it isn't getting?
- What am I avoiding and what is underneath that?
- Where did I show up for myself this week?

These are not assignments. They are doors.

---

### Content Maintenance Ritual (Weekly · 10 minutes)

Review this checklist each Sunday during the weekly reset:
- Cat medication accuracy (any vet changes?)
- Financial deadline dates (any new or shifted?)
- Anchor priorities (still current order?)
- Routine changes needed based on how the week felt

Flag anything that needs a code update for the next session.

---

### Philosophy Alignment Review (Quarterly)

Once per quarter, review the app against its core principles:
- Has any new feature introduced a metric, score, or completion rate?
- Does any wording now imply "you should have done this"?
- Has any content drifted from your original voice?
- Are any interactions now engagement-optimized rather than friction-reducing?

Write a short note. Not a dashboard.

**Hard-day version:**

1. Get out of bed
2. Get cat meds done by 10am

- If you can't journal → sit with your coffee. That counts.

## §10 Rhythm

Your weekly rhythm is a map, not a schedule.
Each day has a focus. The focus is a lens, not a requirement.

| Day | Focus | Anchor |
|-----|-------|--------|
| Monday | Portfolio work | Deep work block |
| Tuesday | Notion R&D + life admin | Dual-mode day |
| Wednesday | Notion R&D + portfolio work | Dual creative day |
| Thursday | Buffer / life admin | Outside time at 2pm is protected |
| Friday | Systems work (digital garden, task board, creative curriculum) | Infrastructure day |
| Saturday | Creative exploration (sculpting, moodboards, galleries, writing, films) | Optional Buddhism class 9am |
| Sunday | Life planning reset (taxes, finances, scheduling, therapy outreach) | Weekly setup 7pm |

### What "focus" means

The focus label tells you what kind of energy this day asks for.
It does not tell you to ignore everything else.

If today is Monday (Portfolio) and an urgent admin task comes up:
- Note it in the capture stack
- Handle it during Thursday buffer
- Return to portfolio work

The focus protects your deep work. Everything else gets scheduled, not abandoned.

### Protected blocks

These blocks are non-negotiable regardless of day:
- 7:30am: Morning Routine (daily)
- 9:00am: Cat Morning Meds (daily)
- 2:00pm Thursday: Outside Time
- 9:00pm: Evening Routine + Cat Evening Meds (daily)
- 9:30pm: Bedtime Meds (daily)

Everything else is modular. Tasks are modular. Everything except cat meds, your meds, and financial deadlines can be rescheduled.
